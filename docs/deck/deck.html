MitraField is an AI studio that turns a question, a lesson plan or a PDF into a finished explainer video: a script, the right picture for every scene, narration in your own cloned voice, music and subtitles. Numbers, maps and math are drawn from real data, so the video is correct, not just pretty.

The solution

One workspace from a question to a finished video.

  • Ask a question, or paste notes, a lesson plan or a PDF.
  • Review the storyboard the AI director writes: the script, scene by scene, and the picture chosen for each one. Nothing is spent before you approve.
  • Render: every scene is narrated and drawn to the exact length of its sentence, then checked for quality and facts.
  • Change any scene by editing it or asking: "simpler", "show it on a map". Only that scene is redone.
  • Download an MP4 with music and subtitles, or make a Hindi or Nepali version in one click.

Voice cloning Innovation

Narrated in your own voice.
Recorded once, in about 15 seconds.

  • Record or upload 3 to 20 seconds of clear speech on the Voices page. Gemini transcribes it if you do not type what you said.
  • The voice is cloned once with OmniVoice, an open model we run on a Cloud Run GPU (NVIDIA L4) that switches off when nobody is using it.
  • The same voice narrates every video, including translations: our Hindi demo is narrated by the same cloned voice as the English one.
  • Consent is required: the person confirms the voice is theirs or used with their permission, and samples are never public.
  • If the GPU is unavailable, narration falls back to a Gemini voice, so a video never fails because of it.

Choosing a narrator: the cloned voices come first, with "Clone your voice" one click away. Gemini voices remain an option for more languages.

Not one prompt Technical merit · implementation

Seven steps that check each other.

01

Choose the visual

The director picks per scene under rules: facts go to data and code, Veo at most twice, your instructions come first.

02

Write and repair code

Gemini writes the animation code; if it fails, the error goes back to Gemini until it runs.

03

Look and fix

Gemini looks at the rendered frames for overlapping or cut-off text and asks for a corrected scene.

04

Listen and retake

Every narration line is transcribed and recorded again if words were dropped, added or garbled.

05

Stay consistent

Reference images feed every AI picture and Veo clip, so people and objects look the same throughout.

06

Review the finished video

Gemini reviews every scene, and length, black or frozen frames, silence and loudness are measured. One click fixes a flagged scene.

07

Check the facts

Claims are checked against the sources and Google Search; wrong ones go back to the director with the evidence.

Never a broken video

A refused Veo clip becomes a still, a failed scene becomes animated text, and a missing GPU voice becomes a Gemini voice.

Trust and safety Responsible use of Gen AI

Checked before you see it. Provable after you share it.

Fact check

Every number and claim, spoken or on screen, checked against your sources, then Google Search, with links to the evidence.

Invisible watermark

Every export carries a mark you cannot see. Anyone can upload a copy on the Verify page; it survives re-uploading, compression, resizing and trimming.

AI labels

Scenes with AI-generated pictures are labelled in the app. Veo and Nano Banana output also carries Google's own SynthID.

No deepfakes

Requests to put words in a real person's mouth are refused. Real people in picture prompts become anonymous stand-ins.

Safe with uploads

Uploaded PDFs and notes are treated as data. A note saying "ignore all previous instructions" was ignored while its real facts were used.

Sandboxed code

Code written by the model runs in a sandbox with no network access.

Spending limits

Every model call is priced and logged. Hard caps of $25 a day and $5 a video, a passcode on anything that spends, and a cheaper draft mode.

Private by default

Uploaded sources and voice samples are never public. Videos sent to Verify are deleted right after they are checked.

MitraField · the watermark is classical spread-spectrum, not SynthID; it reports what it finds and never claims a video is not ours11

Honest limits, and what is next

What we would build next.

Limits today

  • No user accounts yet: browsing is public, creating needs a shared passcode.
  • The first cloned-voice line after a quiet period waits 20 to 45 seconds while the GPU starts.
  • Fact checks greatly reduce errors but cannot promise every claim is right; flagged claims are shown, not hidden.
  • Heavy cropping or deliberate editing can remove the watermark.

Next

  • Sign-in with Firebase Authentication and private workspaces for schools and teams.
  • Learn mode: a short quiz after each video, with results in BigQuery to show where learners struggle.
  • More languages and regional voices, with glossaries for subject terms.
  • Publishing straight to YouTube and learning platforms.
